Arcade of Horror Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Arcade of Horror is a collection of offline-first arcade games. The app is designed to run locally on your device and does not include third-party ads, analytics SDKs, account sign-in, or social tracking.

Information Stored On Your Device

The app stores game progress such as high scores, selected modes, achievements, and the last-played cabinet using local browser storage on your device. This information is used only to restore your game state and show progress inside the arcade hub.

You can export a JSON backup of your local scores, achievements, upgrades, cabinet preferences, and arcade settings from the hub. Export does not automatically upload the file. You choose where to keep or share it. Import replaces local app saves; the app retains a previous local copy so you can undo the last import or cabinet reset.

Information We Collect

We do not sell or share personal information from the app. Local save data remains on your device unless you clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or use device-level backup features controlled by your operating system.

National Leaderboards

Personal bests and score history stay private on your device. If national leaderboards are enabled for a published release, the app may submit only a public display name, game identifier, score, anonymous player ID, and country or region code so the score can appear on the national board. No personal local score history is uploaded.

Network Access

The app bundle is intended to work without external content services unless the optional national leaderboard endpoint is configured for release. Network access may also be used by the platform to load or update the app package, depending on the Android wrapper and store distribution method used for release.

Children

Arcade of Horror is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email listed on the Google Play Store listing for Arcade of Horror.
